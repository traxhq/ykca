Step-by-Step Training Pedagogy

Structured European Step Method curriculum breaking down chess mastery into tested stages.

Step 1 • Grassroots

Fundamentals & Board Navigation

Piece movement rules, pawn promotions, legal castling, square coordinates, notation reading, and single-move checkmate recognition.

Step 2 • Tactics Core

Double Attacks & Tactical Motifs

Tactical calculation motifs: forks, skewers, absolute and relative pins, defending against mating threats, and basic 2-move tactical combinations.

Step 3 • Strategy Intermediate

Vulnerability & Defense Mechanics

Discovered attacks, tactical decoys, clearance sacrifices, pawn endgame fundamentals, and active king positioning.

Step 4 • Advanced Positional

Positional Play & Deflection

Overloading pieces, open file exploitation, weak pawn structures, piece coordination, and advanced transition techniques into endgames.

Step 5 & 5+ • Master Level

Tournament Calculation & FIDE Prep

Master-level calculation depth, opening repertoire construction, deep tactical sacrifices, dynamic defending, and preparing students for official FIDE rated tournaments.
